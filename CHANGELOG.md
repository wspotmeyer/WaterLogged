# Changelog

Notable changes to WaterLogged, by release.

## Unreleased

- Backup and Export to UDDF now confirm when the file has been saved. On iPhone and iPad, Save to Files… replaces the share sheet as the main way to save (Share… is still there for AirDrop and Mail), and the tool shows and announces where the file was saved. On Mac, the save panel shows the same confirmation.
- On Mac, the Home map no longer blurs across the whole screen in full screen. The blurred band behind the tab strip is also gone in a normal window.
- The Dives Per Year chart can now show dives, bottom time, or trips per year, using the same metric picker as the other statistics charts.
- VoiceOver support throughout the app: the dive list, list rows, detail pages, entry forms, statistics charts, the dive profile chart, the Home map and summary, Tools, the dive computer screens, and the Home Screen widgets all have spoken labels and read as single, meaningful items. Ratings are adjustable by swiping up or down, detail page sections appear in the headings rotor, and photos can be reordered with VoiceOver actions.
- Gas switches are now linked directly to their gas mix. Previously, UDDF export and Backup could point a gas switch at the wrong mix on dives with more than one gas.
- Signatures are visible while signing in Dark Mode. The ink was drawn black on the dark form.
- The dive profile chart shows every line with data by default, and its caption explains that the buttons below the graph turn lines on or off.
- A dive's tags now appear as a scrolling row in its detail page header instead of in a separate section beside Notes.
- The gas mix and equipment detail pages have a Trips section, like the dive site and buddy pages.
- The About screen and the Mac Help menu link to the new Support and Privacy Policy pages on the WaterLogged website. About also shows the source commit next to the build number.
- The max depth field's placeholder shows the depth unit instead of "Dive Number".

Initial release. Internal testing only.
