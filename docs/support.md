Thanks for using WaterLogged! If you need help, here's how to get it.

## Get help or report a problem

Email [waterlogged@wspot.net](mailto:waterlogged@wspot.net), or, if you have a GitHub account, [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/wspotmeyer/WaterLogged/issues). It helps to include:

- Your device (for example, iPhone 17 or MacBook Air) and its iOS, iPadOS, or macOS version
- The WaterLogged version and build number, shown on the **About** screen
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
- For dive computer problems: the make and model of your dive computer

Feature ideas are welcome too.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which dive computers are supported?

WaterLogged can download dives from more than 100 Bluetooth dive computers, using the open-source [libdivecomputer](https://www.libdivecomputer.org) library. To start a download, go to **Tools › Import from Dive Computer**. Put your dive computer in Bluetooth or upload mode before you search for it. If yours isn't found, please get in touch and include the model.

### How do I bring dives over from another app?

Export your logbook from the other app as a **UDDF** file, then open **Tools › Import from UDDF** in WaterLogged. UDDF is the standard file format for dive logs, and most dive logging apps can export it.

### How do I move my dives to another app?

Go to **Tools › Export to UDDF** to create a UDDF file of your logbook. You can then import that file into any app that supports UDDF.

### How do I keep my logbook in sync across my devices?

Turn on **Sync with iCloud** in Settings on each device, signed in to the same Apple Account. Sync is off by default.

### How do I back up my logbook?

Go to **Tools › Backup**. This creates a ZIP archive of your complete logbook, including your photos. To restore it, use **Tools › Restore**. Keep your backups somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive or a computer.

> **Important:** If iCloud sync is off and you delete WaterLogged, your logbook is deleted with it. Make a backup first.

### Does WaterLogged cost anything?

No. WaterLogged is free, with no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, and no account. It is also open source. You can find the code at [github.com/wspotmeyer/WaterLogged](https://github.com/wspotmeyer/WaterLogged).
