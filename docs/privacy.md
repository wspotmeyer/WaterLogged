_Effective October 23, 2026_

WaterLogged is a dive logging app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It is written and published by an individual developer, John Meyer, and it does not collect your data. Your logbook belongs to you.

## The short version

- WaterLogged has no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
- The developer never receives your dives or any other information you enter.
- Your logbook is stored on your own devices. If you turn on iCloud sync, it is also stored in your own private iCloud account.

## What WaterLogged stores, and where

Everything you enter is stored only on your device. That includes dives, depth profiles, dive sites, trips, buddies, equipment, certifications, photos, scanned log pages and signatures. WaterLogged has no server of its own, and nothing is sent to the developer.

### iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on **Sync with iCloud** in Settings, your logbook is stored in your private iCloud database so it can sync between your devices. That data belongs to your Apple Account and is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer cannot see or access it.

### Spotlight and Siri

WaterLogged adds your dives, dive sites, trips, and buddies to your device's on-device search index. This lets you find them with Spotlight and Siri. The index stays on your device and is managed by Apple's operating system.

### Widgets

The Home Screen widgets read your dive statistics from a shared storage area on your device. That data doesn't leave your device.

## Permissions WaterLogged asks for

| Permission | Why it's used |
| --- | --- |
| Bluetooth | To connect to your dive computer and download your dives. WaterLogged only talks to the dive computer you choose. |
| Contacts | To fill in buddy or owner details from a contact **you pick**. Only that contact's details are copied into your logbook. WaterLogged never reads your contact list as a whole. |
| Photos | To attach photos **you select** to dives, sites, buddies, equipment, and certifications. Only the photos you pick are copied into your logbook. |

You can change these permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

## Maps

WaterLogged shows dive sites on maps provided by Apple Maps. When a map is on screen, your device requests map images for the area shown, as with any app that uses Apple Maps. Those requests are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. WaterLogged doesn't track your location.

## Files you export or share

When you export to UDDF, save a backup, or share a photo, the file goes only where you choose to send it, such as Files, AirDrop, or Mail. After that, the file is controlled by you and whoever you share it with.

## Crash reports

WaterLogged contains no crash reporting or analytics code. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's privacy settings, Apple may send the developer anonymous crash reports. You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

## Children

WaterLogged collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

You can delete any record in the app. If you delete the app, its data is removed from that device. If iCloud sync is on, you can also remove the synced copy in your iCloud storage settings.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. You can see every past version in this file's history on GitHub.
