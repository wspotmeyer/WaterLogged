**Deep dive. Zero pressure.**

WaterLogged is a simple, beautiful dive log for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Log your dives, relive them, and keep your logbook truly yours, with no strings attached.

- Download dives from more than 100 Bluetooth dive computers
- Import and export your whole logbook with UDDF, the standard file format for dive logs
- Optional iCloud sync, Home Screen widgets, and Spotlight and Siri search
- Free: no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, no account

## Help and information

- [Support](support.md): get help, report a problem, and read answers to common questions
- [Privacy Policy](privacy.md): WaterLogged doesn't collect your data

WaterLogged is open source. You can find the code on [GitHub](https://github.com/wspotmeyer/WaterLogged).
